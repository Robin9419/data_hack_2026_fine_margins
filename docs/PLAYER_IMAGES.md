# Player Image Candidates — Spengler Cup 2025 Final

Research date: 2026-10-11. Intended use: private internal demo, as confirmed by the repository owner.

## Status

39 of 42 players have direct image candidates. Three remain unresolved: Davyd Barandun, Simon Knak, and Chris Egli. Images are candidates, not a blanket clearance for reuse. Official roster photos have no reuse license established in this research; internal access alone does not establish permission. Commons license metadata is recorded where retrieved.

The roster is derived from event lineup IDs, including both goaltenders. Source metadata associates the candidates with players, but visual identity, image resolution, cropping, and actual image availability still require review. No images have been added to the application.

## Files

- `player-images.json`: machine-readable player IDs, names, jersey numbers, image URLs, source URLs, notes, and rights status.
- `player-images-preview.html`: browser preview of candidate images and their source pages. It loads remote images and needs an internet connection.

## Important Details

- HCD portraits from 2026/27 postdate the final. College uniforms and older action photographs also do not depict the 2025 final.
- Preserve the dataset spelling `Gavin McCartthy`; his official college profile uses Gavin McCarthy.
- The UNH roster uses Marty Lavins for the dataset player Martins Lavins.
- Source jersey numbers may differ from the U.S. selection jersey numbers. Match by player identity, not college jersey number.
- Official HCD portraits are from https://www.hcd.ch/de/teams/mens-team?tab=team.
- Both separate Quinn Finley penalties remain unrelated to this image research; no sporting data has been changed.
