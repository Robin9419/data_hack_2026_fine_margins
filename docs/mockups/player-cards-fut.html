Hockey Performance Cards · FUT-style draft

A draft in the spirit of FIFA Ultimate Team, built for ice hockey: shield with chamfered shoulders, rating and position on the left, cut-out portrait, six sub-ratings under the name. Hockey touches: faint rink markings and skate scratches as texture, team jersey stripes across the chest, and a skewed jersey-number plate. All values are real data from the final (31.12.2025).

Tiers

One player, four tiers. The tier sets the frame and the colours, exactly like the match rating tiers in the app (Bronze below 80, Silver 80-89, Gold 90-96, Elite 97+). Elite gets a rotating ice-holo frame.

Players of the match

Real profile cards of the final, each with its own archetype line and the six sub-ratings (percentile among the 40 skaters, 40 to 99).

Performance specials

Dark cards with glitter and an accent colour per special, as in the current design.

Base card and goalkeeper

The base card stays plain: no rating, no stats, a lightly filled dot as rarity. Goalkeepers get three goalie ratings instead of six.

Sub-ratings

  • SPD top speed and sprint distance. SHO goals, expected goals, shots. PAS assists, passes, pass success. CTL puck control share, expected possession value and distance carried forward. DEF blocked shots and puck battles won. PHY time on ice and distance skated.
  • Goalkeepers: SAV save percentage (80 % to 95 %), HDS high-danger save rate, XGS goals saved above expected.
  • Each sub-rating is 40 + 59 x the weighted percentile among all skaters of the game, the same scale as the match rating. Generated by backend/scripts/fut_mockup_data.py.
